## Instructions

- Total Marks: 15
- This is an open-book assignment.
- This assignment builds on Assignment 1.

- Submit:
  - PDF report (3-5 pages)
  - Code notebook (PennyLane / Qiskit)
- Recommended constraint: use <= 6 qubits.

## Objective

To apply a quantum machine learning approach to a classical problem and analyze its feasibility.

## 1. Problem Recap (2 Marks)

- What is the problem?
- Why is it difficult classically?

## 2. Data Representation (3 Marks)

- What are the input features?
- How are they encoded into quantum states?
- Which encoding method is used (angle / amplitude / basis)?

## 3. Quantum Model Design (4 Marks)

Choose one:

- Variational Quantum Circuit (VQC)
- Quantum Kernel Method

Also include:

- Draw the circuit architecture.
- What gates are used?
- How is entanglement introduced?
- What is measured?

## 4. Implementation and Results (3 Marks)

Minimum reporting checklist (compulsory):

- Circuit diagram (clearly labeled)
- Description of dataset used (size, features)
- At least one of the following plots:
  - Measurement probability distribution
  - Loss vs iterations (for VQC)
  - Kernel matrix visualization (for kernel method)
- Final output/result (for example, prediction, classification accuracy, or kernel similarity)

Additional expectations:

- Brief explanation of what each plot shows
- Interpretation of results, not just screenshots

Note: the above is a minimum requirement. Additional plots, experiments, or analysis may be included if relevant.

## 5. Discussion and Limitations (3 Marks)

- Does the quantum approach provide any advantage?
- What are the limitations of your approach?
- How do NISQ constraints (noise, qubits, depth) affect your results?

## 6. References

Provide references used.
